# LP365-P shutoff review

The manufacturer’s manual distinguishes the local protection setting from remote app access. When protection is enabled, the device can detect leaks and close its own valve. Losing Wi-Fi alone does not request closure; the manufacturer says local monitoring can continue on its internal battery. HA cannot provide phone messages while HA/network access is unavailable. This integration does not verify battery condition or mechanical valve operation.

The active mode is chosen from the smart-mode selection in Automatic, or the pro-mode selection in Advanced. Stored pro settings do not establish that Advanced is active. The manual documents adjustable continuous-flow time/volume for modes 4–6, but the device’s raw settings/codes have not all been verified against this firmware’s app display. They are not translated into guaranteed shutoff thresholds.

The alarm-clear procedure in the manual can leave protection disabled until it is re-enabled. After using the app/panel to clear an alarm, check the protection indicator. The manual also says the allowed-flow setting can ignore continuous flow below its limit. The integration exposes the reported minimum-flow function and its raw code, but that code must not be treated as a threshold in L/h. Confirm the actual cutoff and units in the app before relying on small-leak detection. The integration reports protection disabled and valve changes without changing either state. Its additional flow patterns are observations, not leak diagnoses. No observation rule here triggers water shutoff. Version 0.4.0 adds optional manual Open/Close actions behind a disabled-by-default opt-in and per-action confirmation; these are independent of device protection and do not change its thresholds.

A practical verification is a supervised manufacturer test, arranged while someone can inspect the installation and restore the supply. First review the actual app settings, plumbing arrangement and water-dependent equipment. Follow the current manufacturer procedure with fixtures in its required state; verify the actual water interruption, app/panel indication, restoration and re-enabled protection. A reported closed bit or successful command alone would not prove closure. No physical test has been performed by this project.

Sources: [official support/manual download](https://www.frizzlife.com/pages/lp365-support), [official series manual](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0159/8429/5990/files/LP365_Series_Manual.pdf?v=1784856556), and [Frizzlife UK FAQs](https://www.frizzlife.co.uk/pages/faqs).

Manual control software checks the reported position after a single DP1 write; an uncertain response is surfaced without automatic retry. This is not a mechanical closure test. The dashboard maintenance record is the user’s record of an already completed supervised test, not an automated test result.
